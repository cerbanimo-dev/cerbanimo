# Cerbanimo Japan G.K. Succession Governance Specification

Status: planning specification for review and implementation by Japanese bengoshi / judicial scrivener / tax professionals as appropriate. This file is not an executed Articles of Incorporation amendment, will, trust, or inheritance instrument.

## Intent

Cerbanimo Japan G.K. is intended to remain continuously operable if its sole founder-member dies or becomes unable to manage the company. Founder ownership remains in place during life and capacity. At death, the equity interest should pass through the founder's valid succession plan into the designated long-term stewardship structure to the extent recognized under Japanese law and coordinated with U.S. estate planning.

## Japanese Companies Act implementation points

- Companies Act Article 607 makes death a statutory withdrawal event for a member of a membership company unless the statutory exception is used.
- Companies Act Article 608 permits the Articles of Incorporation to provide that an heir or other general successor succeeds to the deceased member's equity interest. For a single-member G.K., this provision should be treated as mandatory succession infrastructure.
- Companies Act Article 590 makes members responsible for business execution by default unless the Articles provide otherwise.
- Companies Act Article 599 governs representation of membership companies.
- Companies Act Article 914 identifies registered matters for a G.K., including members executing business and the representative member.
- Companies Act Article 915 generally requires registration of changes to registered company matters at the head office within two weeks.

## Required Articles of Incorporation provision

Japanese counsel should include an Article 608 succession provision from formation rather than attempting to add it after an emergency.

Working Japanese drafting concept for counsel review:

> 当会社の社員が死亡した場合には、会社法第608条第1項に基づき、当該社員の相続人その他の一般承継人は、当該社員の持分を承継するものとする。

Working English meaning:

> If a member of the Company dies, the heir or other general successor of that member shall succeed to that member's equity interest pursuant to Article 608(1) of the Companies Act.

This is intentionally a drafting concept, not filing-ready Japanese. Counsel must determine the final wording, especially if the founder's U.S. estate plan uses a trust, multiple fiduciaries, or a non-Japanese holding structure. A foreign trust should not be assumed to function as a Japanese-law member in the same way a U.S. trust may hold an LLC interest. The succession path must identify the actual person or legal structure capable of holding the G.K. interest under Japanese law.

## Single-successor objective

The estate plan and Japanese Articles should be coordinated to avoid accidental fragmentation of the G.K. interest among multiple successors if a single stewardship owner is intended. Japanese counsel should determine the lawful mechanism for identifying one successor owner or fiduciary structure while respecting inheritance law, tax law, and Article 608.

## Operational continuity

Maintain a private Japan Emergency Authority Schedule identifying the local operational contact and alternates. That appointment should not itself transfer equity.

Japanese counsel should define the registered and contractual authority that can remain effective during founder incapacity and during the transition after death. Do not assume that a non-member operational representative can automatically act as the statutory representative member of a G.K.; the registered representation structure must comply with the Companies Act and the filed Articles.

Continuity authority may cover ordinary payroll, vendors, infrastructure, security, customer communications, tax administration, preservation of records, and required government filings. Extraordinary asset sales, disposal of core IP, dissolution, or changes in beneficial ownership should require the authority specified in the executed corporate and estate instruments.

## Death activation checklist

1. Preserve systems, corporate seals and records, repositories, contracts, banking access, and tax records.
2. Verify the heir/general successor and the authority of any executor, trustee, or other fiduciary under the applicable estate documents.
3. Confirm that the Article 608 succession clause is effective for the actual successor structure.
4. Update the member and business-executing-member records as required.
5. File required commercial-registration changes promptly. Article 915 generally sets a two-week period for changes to matters registered under Article 914.
6. Update banks, payment providers, insurers, vendors, tax authorities, and other counterparties only after authority is documented.
7. Review Japanese inheritance-tax exposure and the tax character of the succession.
8. Review all agreements with Cerbanimo US LLC and other national affiliates for succession or change-of-control effects.

## Incapacity activation checklist

1. Verify the founder's incapacity under the executed estate documents and applicable law.
2. Determine what authority is recognized in Japan for the relevant attorney-in-fact, guardian, curator, trustee, or other fiduciary.
3. Preserve ownership unless a valid instrument and applicable law require a transfer.
4. Activate only the authority needed for safe ordinary operations.
5. Maintain an auditable record of emergency actions and registration changes.

## National-branch separation

Cerbanimo Japan G.K. is a Japanese entity affiliated with other Cerbanimo national entities through common founder ownership, agreements, software, branding, and shared governance protocols. No other national branch acquires ownership merely through that affiliation. Any later transfer into a global stewardship or holding structure must be an affirmative documented transaction.
