# Cerbanimo US LLC Succession Governance Specification

Status: planning specification for review and implementation by New York company, estate, and tax counsel. This file is not an executed legal instrument.

## Intent

Cerbanimo US LLC is intended to remain continuously operable if its founder dies or becomes unable to manage the company. Founder ownership remains in place during life and capacity. On death, the membership interest should pass under the founder's executed estate plan to the designated stewardship fiduciary structure. Emergency operating authority should not itself convey beneficial ownership.

## New York implementation points

- NY LLC Law 603 and 604 distinguish assignment of an economic interest from admission as a member. The operating agreement should therefore expressly provide the intended substituted-member process.
- NY LLC Law 608 permits the legal representative of a deceased or legally incompetent member to exercise the member's rights for estate or property administration.
- NY LLC Law 701(a)(4) provides a continuation mechanism if the LLC has no remaining members. The default period is 180 days unless the operating agreement provides otherwise.
- The operating agreement should state that death or incapacity does not require dissolution to the fullest extent permitted by law, pre-authorize the intended successor-admission process, and define objective evidence of fiduciary authority.

## Continuity controls

Maintain a private emergency-authority schedule naming primary and alternate continuity operators. Such operators may preserve ordinary operations, infrastructure, payroll, security, contracts, records, insurance, and tax administration, but should not receive membership ownership merely from that appointment.

Extraordinary asset sales, disposal of core IP, pledging of ownership, dissolution, or admission of a new beneficial owner should require the authority specified in the executed operating agreement and estate plan.

Shared branding, software, infrastructure, or governance between Cerbanimo national entities does not create cross-ownership. Any later global holding or stewardship structure must be created by an affirmative documented transaction.

## Activation checklist

1. Preserve systems, records, repositories, domains, contracts, and credentials.
2. Verify the authority of the estate or incapacity fiduciary.
3. Use the NY LLC Law 701 continuation procedure if required.
4. Admit the estate-plan successor in accordance with the executed operating agreement.
5. Update the membership ledger and required financial, tax, vendor, and governmental records.
6. Review federal and New York tax classification after the ownership transition.
7. Review all cross-border affiliate agreements for succession or change-of-control effects.

## Documents to execute outside this repository

Counsel should coordinate the LLC operating agreement, the founder's estate/trust plan, incapacity authority, membership-transfer mechanism, successor fiduciary provisions, and secure emergency-authority schedule. Do not place executed estate documents, tax IDs, private addresses, passwords, recovery codes, or cryptographic secrets in this public repository.
