# ADR-005 — Founding Commons is a pack

- Status: Accepted
- Decision authority: Sol, executive architecture handoff
- Recorded: 2026-07-22

## Context

The founding scenario is culturally important and technically valuable, but
hard-coding it would turn one founding story into universal kernel law.

## Decision

Houses, founding quests, story copy, shared disruption, and visual atmosphere
use public kernel contracts as a first-party removable pack.

## Consequences

Packet 001 contains no Founding Commons conditional, type, command, or event.

## Reversal strategy

An individual mechanic may enter the kernel only through a general use case and
the constitutional amendment process.
