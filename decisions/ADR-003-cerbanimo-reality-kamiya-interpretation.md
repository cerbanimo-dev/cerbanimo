# ADR-003 — Cerbanimo owns reality; Kamiya owns interpretation

- Status: Accepted
- Decision authority: Sol, executive architecture handoff
- Recorded: 2026-07-22

## Context

A capable companion needs expressive access without hidden mutation authority.
Unstructured model prose cannot serve as a canonical command or receipt.

## Decision

Kamiya may read permitted context and prepare formal proposals. Cerbanimo alone
validates, confirms, executes, and receipts canonical commands.

## Consequences

Every proposed mutation carries a version-bound semantic diff. Model identities
are excluded from canonical command actors.

## Reversal strategy

No direct-model-write design is constitutionally compatible. Proposal tooling
may evolve behind versioned schemas.
